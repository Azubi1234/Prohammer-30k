--- RITE 0: THE MARU SKARA || allegiance=None ---
  The Maru Skara is one of the most celebrated battle doctrines of the Emperor's Children: a carefully orchestrated feint in which the visible strength of the Legion fixes the enemy in place while a concealed blade manoeuvres to deliver the killing stroke.
  EFFECTS
  THE OPEN BLADE
  All units deployed normally at the beginning of the battle form the Open Blade.
  The army's Warlord must form part of the Open Blade and must begin the battle on the battlefield.
  THE HIDDEN BLADE
  Before deployment, nominate up to three Emperor's Children units selected from the following:
  • Legion Assault Squads
  • Legion Veteran Squads
  • Legion Terminator Squads
  • Palatine Blade Squads
  • Phoenix Terminator Squads
  • Legion Bike Squadrons
  • Legion Sky Hunter Jetbike Squadrons
  An Independent Character may accompany a nominated unit normally.
  Dedicated Transports purchased for a nominated unit form part of the Hidden Blade with that unit.
  All units forming the Hidden Blade must begin the battle in Reserve.
  PERFECTLY TIMED STRIKE
  After deployment zones have been determined but before either army deploys, secretly record:
  • either game turn 2 or game turn 3;
  • either the left or right side table edge.
  Do not reveal this choice to the opposing player.
  At the beginning of the chosen Emperor's Children turn, reveal the recorded choice.
  Every surviving unit of the Hidden Blade becomes available automatically without making a Reserve roll.
  These units enter play from the chosen side table edge.
  They may act normally during the turn in which they arrive.
  THE KILLING STROKE
  During the player turn in which the Hidden Blade arrives, all Emperor's Children units may re-roll failed Advance rolls and failed Charge distance rolls. The second result must be accepted.
  LIMITATIONS
  The army's Warlord must begin the battle as part of the Open Blade.
  The Hidden Blade may contain no more than three units, excluding attached Independent Characters and Dedicated Transports.
  The army may include no more than two Heavy Support choices.
  Immobile units may not be selected.
--- RITE 1: 3RD COMPANY ELITE || allegiance=Traitor ---
  TRAITOR ONLY
  As the III Legion descended into corruption, entire formations embraced the experimental sonic augmentations first developed amongst the Kakophoni. The most infamous of these formations was the 3rd Company, whose warriors willingly submitted themselves to increasingly extreme surgical and sonic modification.
  EFFECTS
  CHOSEN OF VAIROSEAN
  Kakophoni Squads may be selected as Troops choices. Kakophoni Squads selected as Troops may fulfil compulsory Troops selections.
  All Kakophoni Squads in the Detachment gain the Relentless special rule.
  SONIC ASSAULT
  Any Emperor's Children Infantry unit composed entirely of models wearing Power Armour or Artificer Armour may purchase Sonic Shriekers for +2 points per model. Every eligible model in the unit must purchase the upgrade.
  A unit equipped in this manner follows the normal Sonic Shrieker rules.
  ARMOURY OF EXCESS
  The normal restriction allowing only one Legion Heavy Support Squad to select Sonic Weaponry is removed.
  Up to two Legion Heavy Support Squads may select Sonic Weaponry when using this Rite of War.
  PERFECT CACOPHONY
  A unit in which every surviving model is equipped with either a Sonic Weapon or Sonic Shrieker gains the Fear special rule.
  LIMITATIONS
  This Rite of War may only be selected by a Traitor Emperor's Children Detachment.
  The army must include at least one Kakophoni Squad.
  The army's Warlord must either:
  • be equipped with a Sonic Shrieker; or
  • be a named Character whose rules specifically associate him with the Kakophoni or 3rd Company.
  The army may include no more than one Fortification.

  The Hammer of Olympia represents the characteristic method of war favoured by Perturabo's Grand Battalions: massed infantry advancing beneath disciplined fire while armoured formations grind inexorably forward alongside them.

  The army's compulsory Troops choices must be selected from:

  Models equipped with Shrapnel Bolts may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 when firing those weapons at an enemy unit within 12".

--- RITE 1: THE IRONFIRE || allegiance=None ---
  The Ironfire is a siege doctrine in which artillery fire is advanced across the battlefield immediately ahead of the Legion's assault formations.
  Iron Warriors infantry advance through explosions, collapsing structures and clouds of debris, trusting their gunners to place each successive salvo only metres ahead of their position.
  EFFECTS
  ARTILLERY COLUMN
  One Legion Artillery Tank Squadron may be selected as a non-compulsory Troops choice.
  An Artillery Tank Squadron selected in this manner:
  • does not occupy a Heavy Support choice;
  • may not fulfil a compulsory Troops selection;
  • does not count as a Scoring Unit unless it would normally do so for another reason.
  The normal 0–1 restriction on Legion Artillery Tank Squadrons still applies.
  ROLLING BOMBARDMENT
  When an Iron Warriors Barrage weapon fires at a point within 12" of a friendly Iron Warriors unit, reduce its scatter distance.
  If the attack would normally scatter 2D6", it scatters only D6" instead.
  After resolving the Barrage attack, place an Ironfire Marker at the final centre point of the Blast marker.
  WALKING THE FIRE
  When another Iron Warriors Barrage weapon fires, it does not scatter if the initial target point is:
  • within 18" of an Ironfire Marker; AND
  • within 6" of a friendly Iron Warriors unit.
  After resolving the attack, place a new Ironfire Marker at the centre of the Blast marker as normal.
  If an Iron Warriors Shooting phase ends without a new Ironfire Marker being placed, remove all Ironfire Markers from the battlefield.
  RIDE THE IRONFIRE
  Iron Warriors Infantry units with at least one model within 6" of an Ironfire Marker gain the Fearless special rule.
  This benefit lasts only while at least one model in the unit remains within 6" of an Ironfire Marker.
  LIMITATIONS
  The army's Warlord must possess the Master of the Legion special rule.
  The army must contain at least one unit equipped with a Barrage weapon.
  If the mission designates one player as the Attacker and the other as the Defender, the Iron Warriors must be the Attacker.
  The army may not include a Fortification.

  EFFECTS
  THE ALEMBIC OF ADAMANT
  Thousand Sons units automatically pass Morale and Pinning tests while they have at least one model within 6" of an Objective.
  If the mission does not use Objective markers, after deployment zones have been determined the Thousand Sons player may nominate one terrain feature outside the enemy deployment zone. The centre of that terrain feature counts as an Objective for the purposes of this rule only.
  THE CAUSTIC OF GRACE
  Thousand Sons Infantry units may enter Overwatch even if they moved during their preceding Movement phase, provided they did not Advance.
  All other ProHammer requirements for entering Overwatch apply normally.
  THE TRANSITION OF VITRIOL
  Thousand Sons models may re-roll failed To Hit and To Wound rolls when attacking an enemy unit which is Falling Back.
  Against Vehicles affected by a mission-specific retreat or withdrawal rule, re-roll failed Armour Penetration rolls instead of failed To Wound rolls.
  LIMITATIONS
  Every Troops choice in the Detachment must be taken at its maximum permitted unit size.
  The Detachment may not contain more Vehicles with the Tank or Flyer type than it contains Infantry units.
  The Detachment may not include a Fortification.
--- RITE 1: THE GUARD OF THE CRIMSON KING || allegiance=None ---
  When the most powerful formations of the XV Legion were committed to battle, the Sekhmet and the Legion's greatest sorcerers could be concentrated into a single overwhelming psychic spearhead.
  EFFECTS
  ASTRAL WARFARE
  The first two psychic powers successfully invoked by Thousand Sons Psykers during each player turn are ignored when determining the penalties caused by Disturbance in the Warp.
  All other ProHammer psychic rules apply normally.
  WREATHED IN LIGHTNING, THEY REND THE VEIL
  All Thousand Sons units composed entirely of models wearing Terminator Armour gain Teleportation Transponders at no additional points cost.
  Thousand Sons Independent Characters may purchase Teleportation Transponders for +10 points regardless of the armour they wear.
  Whenever a unit or Character using these Transponders arrives by Deep Strike, it gains the Fear special rule and may re-roll Invulnerable Save rolls of 1 until the beginning of the next Thousand Sons player turn.
  INITIATES OF THE SCARAB
  Sekhmet Terminator Cabals may be selected as Troops choices and must fulfil the Detachment's compulsory Troops selections.
  THE BIDDING OF THE CRIMSON KING
  Magnus the Red may fulfil a compulsory HQ selection when using this Rite and does not occupy a Lord of War selection.
  LIMITATIONS
  The army's Warlord must be Magnus the Red, Ahzek Ahriman or a Thousand Sons Praetor.
  A Thousand Sons Praetor selected as the Warlord may be upgraded from Mastery Level 2 to Mastery Level 3 for +25 points.
  The Detachment may not contain more Vehicle units than units with the Legiones Astartes (Thousand Sons) special rule.
  The Detachment may not include an Allied Detachment or Fortification.
--- RITE 2: THE FELLOWSHIPS OF PROSPERO || allegiance=None ---
  The Thousand Sons were organised into great Fellowships whose warriors trained and fought beneath circles of powerful psykers. In some formations entire battle companies acted as extensions of their sorcerous commanders, combining Legion discipline with the collective psychic gifts of Prospero.
  EFFECTS
  CIRCLES OF INITIATES
  A Legion Tactical Squad containing 20 models may purchase Brotherhood of Psykers (Mastery Level 1\) for +25 points per squad.
  A Tactical Squad upgraded in this manner selects one psychic power from the normal Psychic Disciplines available to Thousand Sons Psykers and gains the Cult Mastery benefit of its chosen Prosperine Cult.
  The unit follows all normal ProHammer rules for Brotherhood of Psykers.
  FELLOWSHIP VETERANS
  Legion Veteran Squads and Legion Terminator Squads purchase Brotherhood of Psykers for +15 points per squad instead of the normal +25 points.
  MAGISTER TEMPLI
  At the beginning of each Thousand Sons player turn, the Warlord may nominate one friendly Psychic Brotherhood within 12".
  Until the beginning of the next Thousand Sons player turn, that Brotherhood may use the Warlord's Leadership when taking Psychic Tests.
  This specifically overrides the normal rule preventing a Psychic Brotherhood from using the Leadership of an attached Independent Character.
  ORDER OF THE CULTS
  Every Psychic Brotherhood in the Detachment must belong to the same Prosperine Cult as at least one Thousand Sons Independent Character in the army.
  LIMITATIONS
  The army's Warlord must be a Thousand Sons Psyker.
  The Detachment must include at least two Psychic Brotherhoods.
  A Legion Tactical Squad may only purchase Brotherhood of Psykers if it contains 20 models.
  The Detachment may include no more than one Fast Attack choice.
  The Detachment may not include an Allied Detachment.

  RELENTLESS MARCH
  At the beginning of each Sons of Horus player turn, determine the position of the majority of the surviving models in each Sons of Horus Infantry unit. The unit gains the appropriate special rule until the beginning of the next Sons of Horus player turn:
  • Within the Sons of Horus deployment zone — Relentless.

--- RITE 1: THE BLACK REAVING || allegiance=None ---
  The Black Reaving embodies the Cthonian heritage of the XVI Legion. Rather than engaging the enemy in a single ordered battle line, Reaver companies and supporting formations encircle their prey, isolate individual formations and fall upon them from several directions like the gangs of ancient Cthonia.

  REAVER ONSLAUGHT
  Reaver Attack Squads may be selected as Troops choices and may fulfil compulsory Troops selections.
  At least one of the Detachment's compulsory Troops choices must be a Reaver Attack Squad.

  After both armies have been selected, nominate up to two Sons of Horus Infantry units. These units gain the Outflank special rule.
  Any Dedicated Transport purchased for one of these units also gains Outflank and enters play together with its passengers.
  An Independent Character which begins the battle attached to one of these nominated units may Outflank with it.
  When one of the two nominated units enters play, the Sons of Horus player may choose the side table edge from which that unit enters instead of rolling to determine the edge. The other nominated unit follows the normal ProHammer Outflank rules.
  LODGE CEREMONY
  Once per battle, at the beginning of a Sons of Horus player turn, the Warlord may declare a Lodge Ceremony.
  Until the end of that player turn, all non-Vehicle Sons of Horus units gain the Fleet special rule.
  CUT THEM APART
  During the first round of a close combat, a Sons of Horus unit which charged an enemy unit that was already engaged with another friendly Sons of Horus unit may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1.
  This benefit applies only during the player turn in which the unit charged.
  LIMITATIONS
  The Detachment must include a Legion Centurion upgraded to a Master of Signals Consul.
  At least one compulsory Troops choice must be a Reaver Attack Squad.
  The Detachment must include at least as many Fast Attack choices as Heavy Support choices.
  The Detachment may not include a Fortification or an Allied Detachment.

  Before determining which player takes the first turn, the Alpha Legion player must choose one of the following benefits: add +1 to their roll to determine who takes the first turn, or re-roll a failed attempt to Seize the Initiative.

  The Alpha Legion player may re-roll the dice used to determine which player takes the first turn.

  FALSE FLAGS
  During the first Game Turn, an enemy unit which declares a shooting attack against an Alpha Legion unit must first pass a Leadership test unless that enemy unit has already been fired upon by an Alpha Legion unit during the current player turn.
  If the test is passed, the shooting attack is resolved normally. If the test is failed, the unit may not make that shooting attack.
  False Flags does not affect Overwatch, Return Fire or Stand & Shoot attacks.
  LEAVE NO HEAD UPON THE SERPENT
  The Alpha Legion must destroy the enemy Warlord before the end of the battle.
  If the enemy Warlord survives, the opposing player gains an additional D3 × 50 Victory Points.
  This rule has no effect in missions which do not use Victory Points.

  Every Alpha Legion Vehicle must begin the battle in Reserve unless a mission specifically prohibits it from doing so.
  The Detachment may not include an Allied Detachment.
