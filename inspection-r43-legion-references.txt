===== III — EMPEROR'S CHILDREN =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
EXEMPLARS OF WAR
The Emperor's Children seek perfection in every aspect of warfare, placing particular emphasis upon speed, precision and the flawless execution of carefully prepared battle plans.
Emperor's Children units gain the Crusader special rule.
In addition, during an Assault phase in which an Emperor's Children model charges, that model gains +1 Initiative until the end of that Assault phase. This bonus may be combined with other Initiative modifiers.
PURITY ABOVE ALL
The Emperor's Children place extraordinary importance upon the physical purity and perfection of their warriors, and their Apothecaries hold positions of particular importance within the Legion.
Any Legion Veteran Squad may upgrade its Legion Veteran Sergeant to an Apothecary for +25 points.
In addition, up to one Legion Tactical Squad or Legion Assault Squad in the army may upgrade its Legion Sergeant to an Apothecary for +25 points. The Apothecary retains all weapons and wargear previously carried by the Sergeant and is additionally equipped with a Narthecium.
The Apothecary counts as an Apothecary for all rules and wargear restrictions and may purchase a Reductor for +5 points.
MARTIAL PRIDE
The warriors of the Emperor's Children possess an almost pathological desire to prove their superiority in battle. Retreat from an opponent once battle has been joined is regarded as an admission of inferiority few of Fulgrim's sons are willing to accept.
Emperor's Children units may not voluntarily withdraw from close combat.
In addition, if an Emperor's Children unit wins a close combat and all enemy units it was engaged with retreat, the Emperor's Children unit must Pursue if it is able to do so. It may not take a Restraint Test in order to Consolidate instead.
EMPEROR'S CHILDREN ARMOURY
The III Legion has always placed exceptional value upon weapons of perfect balance, craftsmanship and aesthetic refinement.
As the Horus Heresy progressed, the Traitor elements of the Legion supplemented these masterpieces with increasingly experimental and horrific sonic devices.
PHOENIX SPEAR
An Emperor's Children Independent Character or unit Character able to select a Power Weapon may instead select a Phoenix Spear for +20 points. A model already equipped with a Power Weapon as part of its basic wargear may exchange it for a Phoenix Spear for +5 points.
PHOENIX SPEAR — 20 POINTS
Any Emperor's Children Independent Character or unit Character able to select a Power Weapon may instead select a Phoenix Spear for +20 points. A model already equipped with a Power Weapon as part of its basic wargear may exchange it for a Phoenix Spear for +5 points.

DIGITAL LASERS
An Emperor's Children Independent Character may purchase Digital Lasers for +15 points. A model equipped with Digital Lasers adds +1 to its Attacks characteristic. Digital Lasers may not be combined with Terminator Honours.
SONIC SHRIEKER
TRAITOR ONLY
Sonic Shriekers are surgically implanted resonators capable of emitting disorientating waves of sound that overwhelm the senses of nearby enemies. An Emperor's Children Independent Character may purchase a Sonic Shrieker for +10 points. Additional units may gain access to Sonic Shriekers through their unit entry or a Rite of War.
During the first round of a close combat, an enemy model in base contact with a model equipped with a Sonic Shrieker suffers -1 Weapon Skill.
If every model in an Emperor's Children unit is equipped with a Sonic Shrieker, every enemy model engaged with that unit suffers -1 Weapon Skill during the first round of close combat instead.
This modifier is not cumulative. Fearless models are unaffected.

Certain formations of the Traitor Emperor's Children have begun employing experimental sonic weapons developed from the terrible discoveries made during the Legion's descent into corruption.

EMPEROR'S CHILDREN RITE OF WAR:
THE MARU SKARA
The Maru Skara is one of the most celebrated battle doctrines of the Emperor's Children: a carefully orchestrated feint in which the visible strength of the Legion fixes the enemy in place while a concealed blade manoeuvres to deliver the killing stroke.
EFFECTS
THE OPEN BLADE
All units deployed normally at the beginning of the battle form the Open Blade.
The army's Warlord must form part of the Open Blade and must begin the battle on the battlefield.
THE HIDDEN BLADE
Before deployment, nominate up to three Emperor's Children units selected from the following:
• Legion Assault Squads
• Legion Veteran Squads
• Legion Terminator Squads
• Palatine Blade Squads
• Phoenix Terminator Squads
• Legion Bike Squadrons
• Legion Sky Hunter Jetbike Squadrons
An Independent Character may accompany a nominated unit normally.
Dedicated Transports purchased for a nominated unit form part of the Hidden Blade with that unit.
All units forming the Hidden Blade must begin the battle in Reserve.
PERFECTLY TIMED STRIKE
After deployment zones have been determined but before either army deploys, secretly record:
• either game turn 2 or game turn 3;
• either the left or right side table edge.
Do not reveal this choice to the opposing player.
At the beginning of the chosen Emperor's Children turn, reveal the recorded choice.
Every surviving unit of the Hidden Blade becomes available automatically without making a Reserve roll.
These units enter play from the chosen side table edge.
They may act normally during the turn in which they arrive.
THE KILLING STROKE
During the player turn in which the Hidden Blade arrives, all Emperor's Children units may re-roll failed Advance rolls and failed Charge distance rolls. The second result must be accepted.
LIMITATIONS
The army's Warlord must begin the battle as part of the Open Blade.
The Hidden Blade may contain no more than three units, excluding attached Independent Characters and Dedicated Transports.
The army may include no more than two Heavy Support choices.
Immobile units may not be selected.
EMPEROR'S CHILDREN RITE OF WAR:
3RD COMPANY ELITE
TRAITOR ONLY
As the III Legion descended into corruption, entire formations embraced the experimental sonic augmentations first developed amongst the Kakophoni. The most infamous of these formations was the 3rd Company, whose warriors willingly submitted themselves to increasingly extreme surgical and sonic modification.
EFFECTS
CHOSEN OF VAIROSEAN
Kakophoni Squads may be selected as Troops choices. Kakophoni Squads selected as Troops may fulfil compulsory Troops selections.
All Kakophoni Squads in the Detachment gain the Relentless special rule.
SONIC ASSAULT
Any Emperor's Children Infantry unit composed entirely of models wearing Power Armour or Artificer Armour may purchase Sonic Shriekers for +2 points per model. Every eligible model in the unit must purchase the upgrade.
A unit equipped in this manner follows the normal Sonic Shrieker rules.
ARMOURY OF EXCESS
The normal restriction allowing only one Legion Heavy Support Squad to select Sonic Weaponry is removed.
Up to two Legion Heavy Support Squads may select Sonic Weaponry when using this Rite of War.
PERFECT CACOPHONY
A unit in which every surviving model is equipped with either a Sonic Weapon or Sonic Shrieker gains the Fear special rule.
LIMITATIONS
This Rite of War may only be selected by a Traitor Emperor's Children Detachment.
The army must include at least one Kakophoni Squad.
The army's Warlord must either:
• be equipped with a Sonic Shrieker; or
• be a named Character whose rules specifically associate him with the Kakophoni or 3rd Company.
The army may include no more than one Fortification.

===== IV — IRON WARRIORS =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
SIEGE MASTERS
The Iron Warriors are the foremost practitioners of siege warfare amongst the Legiones Astartes, trained to identify structural weaknesses, endure prolonged bombardment and reduce even the strongest fortifications through methodical application of force.
Non-vehicle models with the Legiones Astartes (Iron Warriors) special rule receive +1 to Armour Penetration rolls made against Fortifications, Buildings, Bunkers, Tank Traps and other immobile structures with an Armour Value.
In addition, Iron Warriors models only trigger enemy Minefields on a roll of 6.
An Iron Warriors unit occupying a friendly Fortification or Bunker gains the Stubborn special rule.
This rule provides no benefit while occupying a Fortification belonging to the enemy at the beginning of the battle.
AN ARMY OF STEEL AND WRATH
The Iron Warriors favour overwhelming concentrations of artillery, heavy armour and siege engines, accepting a corresponding reduction in the number of rapid assault formations deployed alongside them.
When constructing an Iron Warriors Detachment, the player may exchange two Fast Attack selections for one additional Heavy Support selection.
This exchange may only be made once.
When using the Standard Force Organisation Chart, an Iron Warriors army may therefore include:
• 0–1 Fast Attack choices
• 0–4 Heavy Support choices
All other Force Organisation limits remain unchanged.
STUBBORN RESOLVE
The Iron Warriors possess a grim refusal to accept defeat, often continuing an engagement long after another commander would have ordered a withdrawal.
If a mission uses a variable game length and the game would normally end, the opposing player may demand that one additional complete game turn be played. Roll a D6. On a roll of 3+, one additional complete game turn is played.
This roll may only be made once. After the additional game turn has been completed, the battle ends automatically.
This rule has no effect in a mission which ends immediately when a particular objective or action is completed once that objective or action has already occurred.
PRELIMINARY BOMBARDMENT
The Iron Warriors routinely precede major assaults with prolonged and precisely coordinated artillery bombardments.
This rule only applies in missions which already use Preliminary Bombardment.
When resolving a Preliminary Bombardment, the Iron Warriors player receives one additional Preliminary Bombardment roll for every full 500 points in the army.
Before any Preliminary Bombardment rolls are made, each additional roll must be assigned to an eligible enemy unit or obstacle.
Several additional rolls may be assigned to the same target, but all rolls assigned to that target must be made together.
Additional rolls may not be assigned after bombardment rolls have begun.
Resolve these additional attacks using the normal Preliminary Bombardment rules.
IRON WARRIORS ARMOURY
The IV Legion has little interest in ornament or ritual craftsmanship. Its arsenals favour weapons and equipment designed for prolonged siege warfare, suppression and the methodical destruction of fortified positions.
SHRAPNEL BOLTS — 5 POINTS PER UNIT
The following weapons carried by models in the unit gain the Pinning special rule: Bolt Pistols, Bolters, Combi-Bolters, Storm Bolters, Heavy Bolters, Twin-linked Bolters and the bolter component of Combi-Weapons.Shrapnel Bolts may not be combined with Special Issue Ammunition, Hellfire Rounds or another special ammunition type.
SERVO-ARM
Any Iron Warriors model with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase a Servo-Arm for +30 points, even if the model is not normally permitted to select Techmarine equipment.
The Servo-Arm follows the normal rules presented in the Legiones Astartes Army List.
A model equipped with a Jump Pack may not purchase a Servo-Arm.
BIONICS
Iron Warriors have long made extensive use of extensive cybernetic reconstruction to return wounded Legionaries to battle.
Any Iron Warriors model with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase Bionics for +5 points instead of the normal +10 points.
Bionics otherwise follow the normal rules presented in the Legiones Astartes Army List.
IRON WARRIORS RITE OF WAR:
THE HAMMER OF OLYMPIA
The Hammer of Olympia represents the characteristic method of war favoured by Perturabo's Grand Battalions: massed infantry advancing beneath disciplined fire while armoured formations grind inexorably forward alongside them.
EFFECTS
THE IRON LINE
The army's compulsory Troops choices must be selected from:
• Legion Tactical Squads
• Legion Breacher Siege Squads
Compulsory Troops choices gain Shrapnel Bolts at no additional points cost.
HAIL OF FIRE
Iron Warriors Tactical Squads and Breacher Siege Squads equipped with Shrapnel Bolts may declare a charge after firing Rapid Fire weapons during the Shooting phase. A unit which does so does not receive the normal +1 Attack bonus for charging during that Assault phase.
A unit which used Fury of the Legion during the same player turn may not benefit from this rule.
FURY OF OLYMPIA
Models equipped with Shrapnel Bolts may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 when firing those weapons at an enemy unit within 12".
This benefit does not apply when firing Snap Shots.
ARMOURED RESOLVE
The following Iron Warriors vehicles ignore Crew Shaken and Crew Stunned results:
• Legion Predator Strike Squadrons
• Legion Vindicator Siege Tank Squadrons
• Land Raiders
• Legion Spartan Assault Tanks
Other results on the Vehicle Damage table apply normally.
LIMITATIONS
The army's Warlord must possess the Master of the Legion special rule.
The army's compulsory Troops choices must be Legion Tactical Squads or Legion Breacher Siege Squads.
No Iron Warriors unit may deploy using Deep Strike.
Units which are required to enter play by Deep Strike may not be selected.
IRON WARRIORS RITE OF WAR:
THE IRONFIRE
The Ironfire is a siege doctrine in which artillery fire is advanced across the battlefield immediately ahead of the Legion's assault formations.
Iron Warriors infantry advance through explosions, collapsing structures and clouds of debris, trusting their gunners to place each successive salvo only metres ahead of their position.
EFFECTS
ARTILLERY COLUMN
One Legion Artillery Tank Squadron may be selected as a non-compulsory Troops choice.
An Artillery Tank Squadron selected in this manner:
• does not occupy a Heavy Support choice;
• may not fulfil a compulsory Troops selection;
• does not count as a Scoring Unit unless it would normally do so for another reason.
The normal 0–1 restriction on Legion Artillery Tank Squadrons still applies.
ROLLING BOMBARDMENT
When an Iron Warriors Barrage weapon fires at a point within 12" of a friendly Iron Warriors unit, reduce its scatter distance.
If the attack would normally scatter 2D6", it scatters only D6" instead.
After resolving the Barrage attack, place an Ironfire Marker at the final centre point of the Blast marker.
WALKING THE FIRE
When another Iron Warriors Barrage weapon fires, it does not scatter if the initial target point is:
• within 18" of an Ironfire Marker; AND
• within 6" of a friendly Iron Warriors unit.
After resolving the attack, place a new Ironfire Marker at the centre of the Blast marker as normal.
If an Iron Warriors Shooting phase ends without a new Ironfire Marker being placed, remove all Ironfire Markers from the battlefield.
RIDE THE IRONFIRE
Iron Warriors Infantry units with at least one model within 6" of an Ironfire Marker gain the Fearless special rule.
This benefit lasts only while at least one model in the unit remains within 6" of an Ironfire Marker.
LIMITATIONS
The army's Warlord must possess the Master of the Legion special rule.
The army must contain at least one unit equipped with a Barrage weapon.
If the mission designates one player as the Attacker and the other as the Defender, the Iron Warriors must be the Attacker.
The army may not include a Fortification.

===== V — WHITE SCARS =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
SWIFT ADVANCE
The warriors of the White Scars place speed and freedom of movement above rigid battle lines, advancing rapidly across the battlefield before the enemy can react.
A White Scars Infantry unit composed entirely of models wearing Power Armour, Artificer Armour or Recon Armour may make a Swift Advance.
During its Shooting phase, the unit may forgo shooting and instead move an additional D6".
A unit making a Swift Advance:
• may move even if it made a Normal Move during the preceding Movement phase;
• is not slowed by Difficult Terrain during this additional movement;
• may not move within 1" of an enemy model;
• may not charge during the same player turn.
Models wearing any form of Terminator Armour or Hardened Armour may not use Swift Advance.
BORN IN THE SADDLE
The warriors of the V Legion are without equal in mounted warfare, controlling their Bikes and Jetbikes as naturally as another warrior might wield a blade.
White Scars models mounted on Bikes or Jetbikes gain the Skilled Rider special rule.
In addition, the following units gain the Hit & Run special rule:
• Legion Bike Squadrons
• Legion Sky Hunter Jetbike Squadrons
The unit may only use Hit & Run if every model in the unit, including any attached Independent Character, is mounted on a Bike or Jetbike.
Legion Attack Bike Squadrons do not gain Hit & Run from this rule.
MOUNTED BROTHERHOODS
Many White Scars Brotherhoods wage entire campaigns from the saddle, their massed Bike formations forming the main battle line rather than merely supporting it.
Legion Bike Squadrons may be selected as Troops choices in a White Scars Detachment.
Legion Bike Squadrons selected as Troops may fulfil the army's compulsory Troops selections.
They are otherwise identical to the Legion Bike Squadron entry in the Legiones Astartes Army List.
WHITE SCARS ARMOURY
The warriors of the V Legion carry weapons and tokens whose origins lie in the ancient traditions of Chogoris, adapted to the needs of the Great Crusade without losing their ritual significance.
POWER GLAIVE — 25 POINTS
Any White Scars Character able to select a Power Weapon may instead select a Power Glaive for +25 points. A model already equipped with a Power Weapon as part of its basic wargear may exchange it for a Power Glaive for +10 points.
At the beginning of each Assault phase, the bearer must choose how the Power Glaive is wielded. The chosen mode is used for the remainder of that Assault phase.

CYBER-HAWK
One White Scars Praetor may purchase a Cyber-hawk for +10 points.
At the beginning of each White Scars turn, place the Cyber-hawk marker anywhere on the battlefield.

• it may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 when making shooting attacks;
• its charge distance against that enemy unit is increased from 6" to 7".
The Cyber-hawk is only a marker. It cannot be attacked, does not block movement or line of sight and has no characteristics of its own.
HORSETAIL TALISMAN
One White Scars Independent Character may purchase a Horsetail Talisman for +25 points.
Only one Horsetail Talisman may be included in an army.
Once per battle, at the beginning of the White Scars Shooting phase, the bearer may reveal the Horsetail Talisman.
The bearer and every friendly White Scars non-vehicle unit with at least one model within 6" may choose to move D6" instead of shooting.
Bike and Jetbike units may also make this movement.
A unit which makes this move:
• may not move within 1" of an enemy model;
• must maintain unit coherency;
• may not charge during the same player turn.
Units which are Falling Back, embarked upon a Transport or locked in close combat may not benefit.

WHITE SCARS RITE OF WAR:
CHOGORIAN BROTHERHOOD
The Chogorian Brotherhood is the archetypal war formation of the V Legion: a rapidly moving host of mounted warriors supported by mobile infantry and reconnaissance elements, striking from unexpected directions before disappearing beyond retaliation.
EFFECTS
RIDE LIKE THE WIND
Legion Sky Hunter Jetbike Squadrons may be selected as Troops choices.
Legion Bike Squadrons and Legion Sky Hunter Jetbike Squadrons may fulfil compulsory Troops selections.
LIGHTNING ENCIRCLEMENT
The following White Scars units gain the Outflank special rule:
• Legion Bike Squadrons
• Legion Sky Hunter Jetbike Squadrons
• Legion Attack Bike Squadrons
• Legion Land Speeder Squadrons
• Javelin Attack Speeder Squadrons
In addition, White Scars Infantry units gain Outflank provided that the unit contains no model wearing Terminator Armour or carrying a Heavy weapon. A Dedicated Transport purchased for a unit deploying by Outflank may enter play with that unit.
MASTER OF THE HUNT
The White Scars player may re-roll Reserve rolls for units in this Detachment. The player may choose to re-roll either a successful or failed Reserve roll. The second result must be accepted.
STRIKE AND VANISH
A White Scars Bike or Jetbike unit which successfully uses Hit & Run may roll one additional D6 for its Hit & Run movement and discard the lowest result.
LIMITATIONS

• a Space Marine Bike; or
• a Jetbike.

WHITE SCARS RITE OF WAR:
THE SAGYAR MAZAN
LOYALIST ONLY
The Sagyar Mazan are warriors condemned by dishonour, disobedience or catastrophic failure. Rather than face execution at the hands of their brothers, these penitents are permitted to seek absolution through death in battle.
They do not fight for survival. They fight so that their deaths may have meaning.
EFFECTS
DEATH SEEKERS
Whenever a non-vehicle White Scars unit from this Detachment is completely destroyed and would award Victory Points to the enemy, roll a D6.
1–3: Resolve Victory Points normally.
4–5: The destroyed unit awards no Victory Points.
6: The destroyed unit awards no Victory Points and the White Scars player instead gains 50 Victory Points.
This roll is made separately for each destroyed unit.
THE SERPENT'S EYE
During the first round of any close combat, non-vehicle White Scars units from this Detachment gain the Fearless special rule. This applies whether the unit charged or was charged.
NOT YET DEAD
Non-vehicle White Scars units gain Feel No Pain (6+). If a unit already possesses Feel No Pain, improve its Feel No Pain roll by one step, to a maximum of 4+.
EBON KESHIG
Ebon Keshig units may be selected as Troops choices. They may fulfil compulsory Troops selections. This rule has no effect until the Ebon Keshig army list entry is used.
LIMITATIONS
SEEK DEATH, DO NOT HIDE FROM IT
No unit in the Detachment may voluntarily begin the battle in Reserve. Units which are required by their own rules to begin in Reserve may not be selected.
NO PATH OF RETREAT
Units in this Detachment may not voluntarily withdraw from close combat. If they win a close combat and the enemy retreats, they must Pursue whenever they are able to do so.
THE PENITENT HOST
This Rite may only be used by a Loyalist White Scars Detachment. The Detachment may not include Jaghatai Khan.The army may not include a Fortification. The army may never contain more Vehicle units than non-vehicle Infantry units.

===== VI — SPACE WOLVES =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
HUNTERS OF FENRIS
The warriors of the VI Legion possess instincts honed by the death world of Fenris and a natural talent for meeting an enemy assault with overwhelming violence.
All non-vehicle units with the Legiones Astartes (Space Wolves) special rule gain the Counter-Attack and Acute Senses special rules.
NO MATTER THE ODDS
Space Wolves refuse to measure victory by numbers and will fight on regardless of how badly the battle turns against them.
Space Wolves units ignore negative Leadership modifiers applied to Break Tests caused by losing close combat.
Other Leadership modifiers apply normally.
BLOOD FEUD
The Space Wolves maintain ancient and bitter rivalries with certain brother Legions.
Space Wolves models always hit Dark Angels and Thousand Sons models on a 3+ in close combat unless they would normally hit on a better result. Dark Angels and Thousand Sons models likewise always hit Space Wolves models on a 3+ in close combat unless they would normally hit on a better result.
LEGION ORGANISATION
The VI Legion is divided into fiercely independent Great Companies, each led by its own Jarl and supported by trusted champions and priests.
A Space Wolves Detachment must include one HQ selection for every full or partial 750 points in the army:
Up to 750 points: 1 HQ
751–1,500 points: 2 HQ
1,501–2,250 points: 3 HQ
2,251–3,000 points: 4 HQ
This replaces the normal minimum and maximum number of HQ selections. The normal restriction on models with Master of the Legion still applies. Space Wolves may not select Legion Chaplain Consuls or Legion Librarian Consuls. Their roles are instead fulfilled by Wolf Priests and Rune Priests.
SPACE WOLVES ARMOURY
The warriors of the VI Legion carry weapons and talismans rooted in the traditions of Fenris. To outsiders many of these objects appear primitive or superstitious, yet their effectiveness in the hands of the Rout is beyond dispute.
FROST WEAPON — 20 POINTS
Any Space Wolves Character with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase a Frost Weapon for +20 points. A model already equipped with a Power Weapon may exchange it for a Frost Weapon for +5 points.
A Frost Weapon may be represented by a sword, axe, claw or similar Fenrisian weapon. Its exact appearance has no additional rules effect.

The Imperial Fists possess an unrivalled understanding of defensive warfare and the construction and reduction of fortifications.

If the Imperial Fists are the defenders in a mission which permits the placement of Fortifications or Obstacles, they may place D3 additional 6" sections of razor wire or tank traps.

Imperial Fists possess an uncompromising determination which can drive them to continue fighting even when withdrawal would be the wiser course.
In a mission with variable game length, when the battle would normally end, the opposing player may demand that one additional complete game turn be played. After this additional game turn has been completed, the battle ends automatically.
This rule has no effect if the mission has already ended because a specific objective or action has been completed.

===== XII — WORLD EATERS =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
BLOOD FRENZY
The World Eaters throw themselves into close combat with a violence unmatched amongst the Legiones Astartes.
World Eaters Infantry, Bike, Jump Infantry and Dreadnought models gain +1 Attack during an Assault phase in which they charged.

Whenever a World Eaters unit wins a close combat, it gains the Rage special rule for the remainder of the battle.
For the purposes of this rule, use the ProHammer 3rd–5th Edition version of Rage.
A World Eaters unit which wins a close combat must Pursue a retreating enemy whenever it is permitted to do so.
If no Pursuit is possible because the enemy was completely destroyed, the unit must Consolidate as directly as possible towards the nearest enemy unit.
WORLD EATERS ARMOURY
The armouries of the XII Legion reflect the gladiatorial traditions of Nuceria and the brutal preferences of Angron's sons. Chainaxes and specialised pit weapons are favoured over more elegant arms, intended to inflict catastrophic wounds at close quarters.

===== XIII — ULTRAMARINES =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
CODEX ASTARTES
The XIII Legion possesses an extraordinarily flexible and disciplined command structure, allowing its forces to divide and reorganise rapidly as battlefield conditions demand.
A Legion Tactical Squad consisting of exactly ten models may be divided into two Combat Squads of five models each.
This decision is made before deployment.
The Sergeant, specialist weapons, heavy weapons, Legion Vexilla and Nuncio Vox may be divided between the two Combat Squads as the Ultramarines player wishes.
After being divided, each Combat Squad counts as a separate unit for deployment, Morale, casualties, scoring and all other purposes.
For Victory Points, each Combat Squad is worth half the original unit's total points value. Any remaining fraction is assigned to the squad containing the Sergeant.
If the original Tactical Squad purchased a Dedicated Transport, it must be assigned to one of the Combat Squads. Only that Combat Squad may begin the battle embarked within it.
FLEXIBLE COMMAND STRUCTURE
The Ultramarines maintain an unusually large and highly organised officer corps capable of directing complex operations without loss of cohesion.
An Ultramarines Detachment may include up to four HQ selections.
The normal limitation on models with Master of the Legion still applies.
In addition, an Ultramarines army begins the battle with one additional Strategy Point.
This Strategy Point follows all normal ProHammer rules.
ULTRAMARINES ARMOURY
The armouries of the XIII Legion reflect the ordered and pragmatic nature of the Ultramarines. Rather than favouring esoteric or excessively specialised weapons, the Legion prizes equipment of proven effectiveness, refined through centuries of warfare and manufactured to exacting standards throughout the Five Hundred Worlds.

===== XV — THOUSAND SONS =====

The Thousand Sons possess an unparalleled concentration of psychic talent amongst the Legiones Astartes, and the study of the Warp permeates every level of the Legion.
All Thousand Sons Independent Characters are Psykers.
Unless they already possess a higher Psychic Mastery Level, they count as Psyker (Mastery Level 1).

• Biomancy
• Divination
• Pyromancy
• Telekinesis
• Telepathy

A Psyker may not use the same psychic power more than once during the same player turn.

The warriors of the Thousand Sons were organised according to the great psychic Cults of Prospero, each devoted to the mastery of a particular aspect of the psychic arts. • Raptora
• Corvidae

CULT MASTERY

Cult Mastery does not require a Psychic Test and does not count as the use of a psychic power.

THOUSAND SONS RITE OF WAR:
THE AXIS OF DISSOLUTION
The Axis of Dissolution is a carefully prepared tactical configuration in which the enemy is drawn against positions selected in advance by the Legion's seers, held in place by disciplined resistance and destroyed as the pattern of battle unfolds.

THE ALEMBIC OF ADAMANT
Thousand Sons units automatically pass Morale and Pinning tests while they have at least one model within 6" of an Objective.

All other ProHammer requirements for entering Overwatch apply normally.
THE TRANSITION OF VITRIOL

===== XVI — SONS OF HORUS =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
CLOSE RANGE BRUTALITY
The Sons of Horus favour overwhelming close-range assaults in which gunfire and blade work become almost indistinguishable.
At the beginning of the Assault phase, a Sons of Horus unit which is otherwise able to charge may fire at the enemy unit it intends to charge.
Models armed with Rapid Fire, Pistol or Assault weapons may fire.
Template weapons may not be used.
These attacks hit only on a 5+, regardless of Ballistic Skill, and never cause Morale or Pinning tests.
Firing in this manner does not prevent the unit from charging, but it must charge the unit it fired upon.
If casualties leave the target outside charge range, or destroy it completely, the Sons of Horus unit may not declare a charge against another enemy unit.
Instead, it moves D6" directly towards the target's current position, or its final position if it was destroyed.
This movement is not a charge and may not bring the unit within 1" of another enemy model.
TIP OF THE SPEAR
The Sons of Horus excel at decapitation strikes and sudden assaults upon the most important point in the enemy line.
Even if the mission does not normally permit Deep Strike, the following may deploy using Deep Strike:
• one Sons of Horus Infantry unit selected from Elites;
• one Sons of Horus Independent Character, either alone or joined by a Command Squad.
These units must begin the battle in Reserve.

The Sons of Horus possess an arrogance born of countless victories and the favour of the Warmaster himself
At the end of the final scheduled game turn, the opposing player may demand that one additional complete game turn be played.

The Detachment may include no more than one unit of each of the following types: Jump Infantry, Jetbike, Skimmer and Flyer.
The Detachment may not include Vulkan.
The Detachment may not include a Fortification or an Allied Detachment.

===== XIX — RAVEN GUARD =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
SURGICAL STRIKE
The Raven Guard specialise in sudden attacks upon carefully identified weak points in the enemy line.
Whenever a Raven Guard unit deploys using Deep Strike, the Raven Guard player may re-roll the Scatter die and any dice rolled for scatter distance. The entire result must be re-rolled and the second result must be accepted.
RAPID REACTION
Raven Guard reconnaissance elements act as the eyes of the Legion, directing incoming reserves towards precisely identified points of weakness.
At the beginning of each Raven Guard turn, if at least one friendly Legion Reconnaissance Squad or Mor Deythan Squad has a surviving model on the battlefield and is not Falling Back, add +1 to all Raven Guard Reserve rolls made during that turn.
This bonus is not cumulative.
SHADOW MASTERS
Reconnaissance formations form an integral part of Raven Guard battle doctrine rather than merely supporting the main Legion line.
Legion Reconnaissance Squads selected in a Raven Guard Detachment lose the Support Squad special rule.
They may therefore fulfil compulsory Troops selections normally. Legion Reconnaissance Squads and Independent Characters may buy Sniper Rifles for 2 points instead of 5points or any price otherwise applicable.
LIMITED VEHICLES
The Raven Guard favour mobility and stealth over the massed deployment of heavy armour.
A Raven Guard Detachment may never include more Heavy Support selections than Fast Attack selections.
Dedicated Transports are ignored when determining this restriction.
RAVEN GUARD ARMOURY
The XIX Legion favours equipment suited to reconnaissance, infiltration and sudden violence at close range. Much of its specialist wargear derives from the technologies of Kiavahr and the traditions of the freedom fighters of Deliverance.
RAVEN’S TALONS — 7 POINTS PER MODEL
Any model in a Raven Guard Legion Veteran Squad equipped with a Bolt pistol and Chainsword may replace both weapons with a
pair of Raven's Talons for +7 points. A Raven Guard Character already equipped with a pair of
Lightning Claws may upgrade them to Raven's Talons for +5 points. Raven's Talons count as a pair of Rending Weapons.
The bearer therefore receives the normal +1 Attack bonus for fighting with two close-combat weapons.
Other units may only select Raven's Talons where specificallypermitted by their unit entry.
FULCRUM HAND CANNON — 10 POINTS
Any Raven Guard Independent Character or squad Sergeant with access to the Space Marine Armoury may replace a Bolt Pistol with a Fulcrum Hand Cannon for +10 points.
A Raven Guard Moritat may replace either or both of his Bolt Pistols with Fulcrum Hand Cannons for +10 points each.

INFRAVISOR — 10 POINTS
Any Raven Guard Independent Character or squad Sergeant with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase an Infravisor for +10 points.
The bearer gains the Night Vision special rule and +1 Ballistic Skill, to a maximum of BS6.
When taking an Initiative test caused by the Blind special rule, the bearer and any unit he has joined count as Initiative 1.
SHROUD BOMBS — 10/20 POINTS
A Raven Guard Independent Character may purchase Shroud Bombs for +10 points. A Legion Reconnaissance Squad, Legion Veteran Squad or Mor Deythan Squad may purchase Shroud Bombs for +20 points per squad.
Shroud Bombs count as Defensive Grenades.
In addition, an enemy unit attempting to charge a unit equipped with Shroud Bombs must first pass a Leadership test. If the test is failed, the charge may not be attempted and the enemy unit may not declare another charge that turn.
Vehicles, models with the Daemon special rule and units containing a model with Night Vision are unaffected by this rule.
CAMELEOLINE
Raven Guard Legion Reconnaissance Squads retain their normal option to purchase Cameleoline from their unit entry.
A Raven Guard Independent Character wearing Power Armour, Artificer Armour or Recon Armour may purchase Cameleoline for +10 points.
Cameleoline grants the Stealth special rule.
TELEPORTATION TRANSPONDERS
Any Raven Guard unit composed entirely of models wearing any form of Terminator Armour may purchase Teleportation Transponders for +15 points per unit. A Raven Guard Independent Character wearing any form of Terminator Armour may purchase them for +10 points.
A model or unit equipped with Teleportation Transponders gains the Deep Strike special rule and may deploy using Deep Strike even if the mission would not normally permit it.
An Independent Character intending to Deep Strike as part of another unit must purchase Teleportation Transponders separately.
RAVEN GUARD RITE OF WAR:
DECAPITATION STRIKE
The Raven Guard do not seek victory through attrition when a single precisely delivered blow will suffice. Commanders, communications centres and strategic assets are identified, isolated and destroyed before the enemy can bring its full strength to bear.
EFFECTS
FOR WHOM THE BELL TOLLS
All Raven Guard units gain Preferred Enemy (Independent Characters).
For the purposes of this rule, an enemy unit containing one or more Independent Characters counts as a Preferred Enemy until all Independent Characters in that unit have either been slain or left the unit.
PREDATORY STRIKE
If the mission requires a roll to determine deployment order, choice of deployment zone or which player takes the first turn, the Raven Guard player may re-roll that roll. The entire result must be re-rolled and the second result must be accepted.
This ability may only be used once per battle.
FURY FROM ABOVE
Raven Guard Legion Heavy Support Squads must select either a Legion Drop Pod or Anvillus Dreadclaw Drop Pod as a Dedicated Transport at its normal points cost. All normal Transport Capacity and deployment restrictions apply.
LIMITATIONS
The Detachment may include no more than one Heavy Support choice.
The Detachment may include no more than one Centurion upgraded to a Consul.
The Detachment may not include a Fortification.
The Detachment may not include an Allied Detachment drawn from another Space Marine Legion.
RAVEN GUARD RITE OF WAR:
LIBERATION FORCE
After the massacre at Isstvan V, Corax and the surviving Raven Guard turned their strength towards worlds oppressed by the Warmaster. These forces combined the Legion’s mastery of asymmetric warfare with local armies inspired by the example of the XIX Legion.
EFFECTS
FREEDOM FIGHTERS
Once per battle, at the beginning of any Game Turn, the Raven Guard player may declare Freedom Fighters.
Until the end of that Game Turn, every model in the Raven Guard Detachment and any allied Imperialis Militia Detachment gains the Zealot special rule.
SLAYERS OF TYRANTS
If the mission awards Victory Points for slaying the enemy Warlord, replace the normal Victory Point award with D3 times its normal value.
For example, if the mission normally awards 50 Victory Points for slaying the enemy Warlord, this Rite awards D3 × 50 Victory Points instead.
LEAD BY EXAMPLE
Models belonging to an allied Imperialis Militia Detachment gain the Fearless special rule while they have at least one model within 6" of a friendly model with Legiones Astartes (Raven Guard).
LIBERATORS
An Allied Detachment drawn from the Imperialis Militia may be included without preventing the use of this Rite of War.
All other normal rules governing Allied Detachments continue to apply.
LIMITATIONS
This Rite of War may only be selected by a Loyalist Raven Guard Detachment.
The Detachment may not include units with the Immobile or Slow and Purposeful special rules.
The Detachment may not include a Fortification.

===== XX — ALPHA LEGION =====
LEGION SPECIAL RULES
MUTABLE TACTICS
An Alpha Legion army must select one of the following Veteran Skills at the point where Warlord Traits are selected for the game. This rule applies to all qualifying non-vehicle units in the detachment with Legiones Astartes (Alpha Legion) for the duration of the game. This does not count towards the number of Veteran Skills a unit may have. All normal restrictions associated with the selected Veteran Skill continue to apply.
• Counter-Attack
• Furious Charge
• Infiltrate
• Move Through Cover
• Siege Specialists
• Tank Hunters
SIEGE SPECIALISTS
A unit using Siege Specialists receives +1 to Armour Penetration rolls against Fortifications, Buildings, Bunkers and other immobile structures with an Armour Value. All other abilities use their normal ProHammer rules.
MARTIAL HUBRIS
The Alpha Legion deliberately seeks complex victories intended to demonstrate its superiority, even when a simpler plan would be more efficient.
In a mission using Victory Points, compare the number of completely destroyed units belonging to each player at the end of the battle.
If more Alpha Legion units have been completely destroyed than enemy units, the opposing player receives an additional D3 x 50 Victory Points.
If both players have lost the same number of units, this rule has no effect.
INFILTRATION NETWORK
Alpha Legion operations are routinely prepared by infiltrators, agents and mortal operatives long before the Legion openly enters battle.
Legion Operative units may be selected as Troops choices in an Alpha Legion Detachment.
Up to two Legion Operative units may be selected without occupying a Troops selection on the Force Organisation Chart.
These units must still be paid for normally and count as units for the purposes of Martial Hubris.
ALPHA LEGION ARMOURY
The XX Legion maintained arsenals suited to infiltration, sabotage and targeted assassination. Much of its specialist equipment was designed not for open battle, but to ensure that when the Alpha Legion finally revealed its hand, the enemy was already compromised.
BANESTRIKE AMMUNITION — 5 POINTS PER MODEL
Alpha Legion Seeker Squads, Legion Veteran Squads and Independent Characters equipped with a Bolter, Foeblaster Boltgun, Combi-Bolter or Combi-Weapon may purchase Banestrike Ammunition for +5 points per eligible model. Every eligible model in a squad must purchase the upgrade or none may do so. When firing Banestrike Ammunition, use the Banestrike profile presented below.
When used with a Foeblaster Boltgun, Combi-Bolter or the Bolter component of a Combi-Weapon, retain the weapon's normal number of shots and firing type but reduce its range to 18", use AP4 and apply the Banestrike special rule.
Seeker Squads retain their normal Special Issue Ammunition and may choose to fire Banestrike Ammunition instead whenever they shoot.
Banestrike Ammunition may not be combined with another ammunition type.

BANESTRIKE
A natural To Wound roll of 6 made with Banestrike Ammunition is resolved at AP3.
POWER DAGGER — 5 POINTS
Any Alpha Legion Character with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase a Power Dagger for +5 points.
A Power Dagger counts as a Power Weapon with the Rending and Specialist Weapon special rules.
Attacks made with a Power Dagger are resolved at -1 Strength.
VENOM SPHERES — 5 POINTS
Any Alpha Legion Independent Character or squad Sergeant with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase Venom Spheres for +5 points. The bearer gains the Hammer of Wrath special rule. Units specifically permitted to purchase Venom Spheres in their own army list entry use the points cost given there instead.
LEGION SABOTEUR — +25 POINTS
One Alpha Legion Seeker Sergeant in the army may be upgraded to a Legion Saboteur for +25 points.
The Saboteur retains the profile, weapons and equipment of the model he replaces.
COORDINATED SABOTAGE
After Mutable Tactics has been selected, the Saboteur's squad may select one additional Veteran Skill from the Mutable Tactics list at no additional points cost.
A skill already granted by Mutable Tactics may not be selected again.
PRE-EMPTIVE STRIKE
After both armies have deployed but before the first turn begins, nominate one enemy unit.
If a non-Vehicle unit is selected, it suffers D6 Strength 4 hits. Armour Saves may be taken normally. If at least one casualty is caused, the unit must immediately take a Pinning test with a -1 Leadership modifier.
If a Vehicle is selected, roll a D6. On a 1–3 there is no effect. On a 4–5 the Vehicle suffers one Glancing Hit. On a 6 it suffers one Penetrating Hit.
A Pre-emptive Strike requires no range or line of sight.
TELEPORTATION TRANSPONDERS
Any Alpha Legion unit composed entirely of models wearing any form of Terminator Armour may purchase Teleportation Transponders for +15 points per unit. An Alpha Legion Independent Character wearing any form of Terminator Armour may purchase them for +10 points.
A model or unit equipped with Teleportation Transponders gains the Deep Strike special rule and may deploy using Deep Strike even if the mission would not normally permit it.
An Independent Character intending to Deep Strike as part of another unit must purchase Teleportation Transponders separately.
ALPHA LEGION RITE OF WAR:
THE COILS OF THE HYDRA
The Alpha Legion rarely wages war according to a single plan. False deployments, corrupted communications, infiltrated enemy formations and concealed reserves form overlapping layers of deception until the foe can no longer distinguish the true attack from the feint.
EFFECTS
SUBTERFUGE
Before determining which player takes the first turn, the Alpha Legion player must choose one of the following benefits: add +1 to their roll to determine who takes the first turn, or re-roll a failed attempt to Seize the Initiative.
The choice must be made before any relevant dice are rolled.
SIGNAL CORRUPTION
Enemy Reserve rolls suffer a -1 modifier.
This modifier applies to normal Reserve rolls but does not affect units which enter play automatically on a specified turn.
THE REWARDS OF TREACHERY
The Detachment may include one Legion-specific unit normally available only to another Space Marine Legion.
The selected unit may not be a Primarch, named Character, Independent Character or a unit specifically marked as Unique.
The unit is selected as an Elites choice and is paid for normally. It retains its normal profile, equipment, options and unit-specific special rules, but replaces its original Legion-specific version of Legiones Astartes with Legiones Astartes (Alpha Legion).
The unit therefore benefits from the Alpha Legion's rules, including Mutable Tactics, but does not benefit from the core Legion rules of its original Legion.
LIMITATIONS
The Detachment must take one additional compulsory Troops choice, for a total of three compulsory Troops choices.
Every Infantry unit in the Detachment must possess Infiltrate or Deep Strike, or must have access to and purchase a Dedicated Transport.
If an Infantry unit relies upon Mutable Tactics to satisfy this requirement, Infiltrate must be selected as the army's Mutable Tactic for that battle.
The Detachment may include no more than one Centurion upgraded to a Consul, although Vigilator Consuls do not count towards this restriction.
The Detachment may not include a Fortification or an Allied Detachment drawn from another Space Marine Legion.
ALPHA LEGION RITE OF WAR:
HEADHUNTER LEVIATHAL
The elite Headhunter cadres of the XX Legion were employed to remove commanders, communications officers and other strategically vital individuals before the enemy understood that a battle had even begun.
EFFECTS
HEADHUNTER ELITE
Headhunter Kill Teams may be selected as Troops choices and must be used to fulfil the Detachment's compulsory Troops selections.
Additional Headhunter Kill Teams may also be selected as Troops choices.
SUDDEN STRIKE
The Alpha Legion player may re-roll the dice used to determine which player takes the first turn.
The entire result must be re-rolled and the second result must be accepted.
FALSE FLAGS
During the first Game Turn, an enemy unit which declares a shooting attack against an Alpha Legion unit must first pass a Leadership test unless that enemy unit has already been fired upon by an Alpha Legion unit during the current player turn.
If the test is passed, the shooting attack is resolved normally. If the test is failed, the unit may not make that shooting attack.
False Flags does not affect Overwatch, Return Fire or Stand & Shoot attacks.
LEAVE NO HEAD UPON THE SERPENT
The Alpha Legion must destroy the enemy Warlord before the end of the battle.
If the enemy Warlord survives, the opposing player gains an additional D3 × 50 Victory Points.
This rule has no effect in missions which do not use Victory Points.
LIMITATIONS
Every Alpha Legion Vehicle must begin the battle in Reserve unless a mission specifically prohibits it from doing so.
The Detachment may not include an Allied Detachment.
